Preview of Project Vagrant, an independent, unofficial Vagrant Story runtime.
The launch splash and window/application icons use the shared WoL engine artwork.
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix. Original game content and
branding belong to their respective rights holders.

Downloads: Linux x86_64, Windows x86_64, and universal macOS (Intel/Apple Silicon).
Each ZIP includes the compiled game, vendored engine, self-contained Godot/.NET
runtime and third-party notices. Extract the whole ZIP and keep its files together.
The adjacent .zip.sha256 files provide download checksums.

You must supply your own US Vagrant Story disc (SLUS-01040) as BIN/CUE. Installation
verifies its files locally. No retail game assets, disc images, BIOS, original-game fonts, music, movies,
dialogue, manual or installed-disc cache are supplied in these packages.

The opening movie and the ending music play natively from the installed disc files; FFmpeg is no longer bundled.
This preview vendors the audited Linux, Windows and macOS engine SDKs from the
public wol-engine-release repository, including the dialogue sizing repair.
Loose engine shader implementation files and owned debug symbols are excluded.
The opening fixes cover attached sword poses, guard death completion, Ashley's
walk-to-door animation, door overlap and enhanced dragon-head shading. The
audio-stall, shared hit-resource and STR/XA installation repairs are retained.
macOS previews are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized. This is preview software;
visual fidelity and retail parity remain subject to the limitations in the README.
