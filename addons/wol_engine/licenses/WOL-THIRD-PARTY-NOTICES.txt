native/src/surface_queries.cpp derive from Godot Engine 4.3:
https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.3/core/math/geometry_3d.h
(ray_intersects_triangle and get_closest_point_to_segment).

PlayStation SPU DSP (native/src/ps1_spu)
From the Noah project (https://github.com/yaz0r/Noah), checkout d725fe231e56a895c17df0554042b9e2bd1acb78,
originally MAME's SPU emulator by pSXAuthor, adapted by R. Belmont.
Licensed under the BSD 3-Clause License; the full notice is in native/src/ps1_spu/LICENSE.
Used only by NativePs1AudioSandbox for install-time audio conversion.
Local changes: the current reverb setting is per SPU instance instead of shared by all instances, and the current
transfer address can be read.
